Grading Template

The following formulas will be used to compute a grading score for each company in this game. Go to "List all companies in this game" to see the results of these formulas as applied to each of your companies.

Implementation Note

Custom formula editing is not active in this version of the simulation. Companies are scored using 4 standard categories:

  • Financial Performance — Current cash on hand
  • Bidding Activity — Total bids placed minus disqualified bids
  • Job Success — Total number of jobs awarded
  • Project Completion — Total number of jobs completed

The formula editor below is preserved for reference and future implementation.

Available Formula Codes

The following codes may be used to construct a formula:

  • TOTAL_JOBS - The total number of jobs that have been created so far in this game.
  • COMPANY_BIDS - The total number of bids that the company has made so far.
  • COMPANY_DISMISSED_BIDS - The total number of bids that the company has made that have been rejected for a technical reason.
  • COMPANY_JOBS - The total number of jobs that the company has been awarded (includes both bid and negotiated jobs).
  • COMPANY_AUTO_JOBS - The total number of jobs that the company has been automatically awarded by the admin.
  • COMPANY_PUBLIC_JOBS - The total number of public jobs that the company has been awarded.
  • COMPANY_PRIVATE_JOBS - The total number of private jobs that the company has been awarded.
  • COMPANY_HEAVY_JOBS - The total number of heavy jobs that the company has been awarded.
  • COMPANY_CLOSED_BID_JOBS - The total number of private jobs awarded through closed bidding.
  • COMPANY_NEGOTIATED_JOBS - The total number of private jobs awarded through negotiation.
  • COMPANY_EQUITY - Company's current value for equity.
  • SUM_SIX_CAM_VALUES - Sum of a Company's six CAM scores.
  • COMPANY_JOBS_IN_PROGRESS - The total number of jobs a company has "in progress" at this time.
  • COMPANY_JOBS_COMPLETED - The total number of jobs a company has completed so far.
  • TOTAL_GROSS_MARGIN_JOBS_IN_PROGRESS - The estimated total gross margin for all jobs "in progress."
  • TOTAL_GROSS_MARGIN_JOBS_COMPLETED - The total gross margin for all completed jobs.
Examples:
COMPANY_EQUITY

Grade is equal to company's equity value

COMPANY_JOBS * 2 + COMPANY_BIDS

Grade is equal to twice the number of jobs the company has been awarded, plus the number of bids the company has made.

(COMPANY_EQUITY / 100000) * (COMPANY_BIDS - COMPANY_DISMISSED_BIDS) * COMPANY_JOBS

Grade is equal to equity divided by 100,000, times the number of bids the company made that weren't dismissed, times the number of jobs won.

(COMPANY_EQUITY / 100000) * (COMPANY_BIDS - COMPANY_DISMISSED_BIDS) * COMPANY_JOBS * SUM_SIX_CAM_VALUES

Same as above, multiplied by the sum of a company's six CAM scores.
