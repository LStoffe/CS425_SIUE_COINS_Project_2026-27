Automatically Add Jobs to Every Company

Implementation Note

Auto-awarding jobs directly to companies is not available in this version of the simulation. Jobs are generated automatically each period advance based on game parameters and are available for companies to bid on. This form is preserved for reference and future implementation.

Leave as -1 to let COINS choose Percent Markup (random number between 10 and 20 percent)

Your choice for percent markup may be between 0 and 100.

Leave as 0 to let COINS choose the Job Type

  • 1 = Apartment Building
  • 2 = School Building
  • 3 = Office Building
  • 4 = Hospital
  • 5 = Industrial Building

Leave as 0 to let COINS choose the Job Size

Job Size may range between 10,000 and 99,000 units. Current default "median" values are:

  • Apartment Buildings - 22,000
  • School Buildings - 37,000
  • Office Buildings - 35,000
  • Hospitals - 43,000
  • Industrial Buildings - 44,000

Jobs to be added to each team with a B license

COINS can give each team more than one job that meets these specifications (max = 10).
