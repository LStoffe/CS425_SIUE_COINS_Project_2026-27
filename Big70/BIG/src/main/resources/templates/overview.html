COINS

Construction Industry Simulation

A comprehensive computer simulation of realistic construction business environment where participants compete as contractors in a dynamic market.

Game Summary

COINS is a computer simulation of a realistic business environment where participants play as contractors, competing in a market with variable demand for construction work. Players must set up a complete business strategy, including:

  • Examine available information
  • Determine the best portfolio of jobs to bid on
  • Create strategies to improve bonding limits
  • Set strategies to create negotiated work
  • Develop bid prices for desired jobs
  • Monitor financial position as work progresses
  • Improve company appraisal metrics
  • Choose and modify construction methods
  • Interpret competitors' strategies
  • Respond to changing conditions

Available Job Types

Each period, the simulation generates a list of available jobs and an Estimated Time and Cost Report for each. Companies must decide which jobs to bid on, the bid price, and which construction methods to use for each activity.

  • Apartment Buildings Residential projects with multiple units
  • School Buildings Educational facilities and campus construction
  • Office Buildings Commercial office spaces and corporate facilities
  • Hospital Buildings Healthcare facilities and medical centers
  • Industrial Plants Manufacturing facilities and industrial complexes

Construction Activities & Methods

Each period the simulation generates a list of jobs available for bidding and creates an Estimated Time and Cost Report for each job. Using the this information, each company must decide which jobs to bid on, the bid price, and which of the five methods to use for each of the activities.

Each job will have up to nine activities such as:

  • Excavation Site preparation and earthwork
  • Foundation Structural base construction
  • Basement Below-grade construction
  • Framing Structural framework assembly
  • Closure Building envelope completion
  • Roof Roofing system installation
  • Siding Exterior wall covering
  • Finishing Interior completion work
  • Mechanical & Electrical MEP systems installation

Every activity has five different construction methods that vary in time and cost. The fifth method is generally the use of a subcontractor. All five methods of activity #9 (Mechanical and Electrical) are generally subcontracted. The Estimated Time and Cost Report gives labor and material costs and the amount of time required for every activity using each of the five methods.
Work scheduling is very important in the selection of the methods so projects can be completed by the contractual deadlines, and the costs reduced as much as possible. Each bid price submitted should cover all the firm's direct and indirect job expenses, its main office overhead costs, and the desired profit.

Bidding Rules & Evaluation

The lowest bid will not necessarily win since the computer takes into account several other factors. Companies must monitor financial situations, progress billings, and may request consulting services at a cost.

  • Cash Liquidity Firm's cash-on-hand must be adequate to provide enough liquidity with regard to the bid price.
  • Minimum Bid Bids below the minimum amount are disregarded as irresponsible and rejected.
  • Competition Bid Unknown contractors add competition and ensure realistic bid prices.
  • Bond Limits Firm must be within its bonding limits to be considered.

Consulting Services

Participants can request information on weather forecasts, material prices, labor and material availability, and market projections. These consulting services have a cost and are charged against the firm's financial account.

Progress Reports

Reports provide participants insight into work progress, expenses, financial status, and the impact of uncertainties each period. Players use these reports to manage their business effectively.

  • Work Progress Track progress on each job during the period.
  • Expenses Review expenses incurred for each activity.
  • Uncertainties Monitor the impact of weather, labor, and material changes.
  • Financial Report End-of-period report including cash-on-hand and metrics.

Strategic Adjustments

Firms evaluate methods chosen, adjust strategies for next periods, select faster or cheaper methods, and may use overtime. Liquidated damages must be monitored per project. Loans are available and amortized over one year.

Company Appraisal Metrics

Company metrics provide a snapshot of your firm's overall performance, financial health, and reputation in the market.

  • Financial Liquidity Firm has adequate cash-on-hand for operations and bids.
  • Financial Success Measure of profit and overall financial performance.
  • Responsibility Compliance with project obligations and quality standards.
  • Pace Speed of completing projects efficiently.
  • Ethics Adherence to ethical business practices.
  • Name Recognition Reputation and brand awareness in the market.

Closing Results & Insights

The simulation provides a closing financial report, expected results of ongoing projects, final equity, and team performance metrics. Maximizing profit and net worth are key evaluation criteria.

Final Equity Total company value at simulation end
Performance Metrics Financial ratios and appraisal metrics
Success Criteria Profit maximization and net worth
