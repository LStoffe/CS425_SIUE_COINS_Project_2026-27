Conditions of Loan

  • • The maximum loan amount that can be requested for any one loan is $10,000,000
  • • Loans will be automatically repaid in 7 equal installments over the next 7 periods after the loan is received. You also have the option to payoff the entire outstanding loan balance at any time. If you choose this option, the entire loan balance will be deducted from your cash-on-hand during the next period update and you will be charged no further interest.
  • • An interest rate of 12% annual, or 2.00% per period, will be charged on any unpaid balance.
  • • You are limited to 3 active loans at any one time.
  • • There is only one loan request allowed each period. If you take a second loan request during a period, it will replace your first request.
  • • The loan amount will be added to your cash-on-hand at the beginning of the next period before bids are processed or work done on jobs. Repayments will begin the period after that.
