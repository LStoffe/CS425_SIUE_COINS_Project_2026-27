1.9 Available Jobs

Every period there will be new jobs available for bid. To view public jobs available for the current period, select "Bid on a job" from the Homepage.

The list of available public jobs provides the following information:

  • Job number (jobs are numbered sequentially in the order they are created)
  • Type of job
  • Size of the job stated in construction units
  • Liquidated damages per day
  • Completion deadline
  • Your current bid (if any)
  • Links for more information or to bid on this job

Lists of available private jobs are not posted anywhere. If a private customer wants you to negotiate or bid on one of their jobs, they will contact you directly by sending a message to your inbox.
