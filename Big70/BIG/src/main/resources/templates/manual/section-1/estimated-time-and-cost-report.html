1.10 Estimated Time and Cost Reports

The Estimated Time and Cost Report gives an analysis of the five different methods of construction for each activity in a job. This report also shows each job's retention policy and gives the amount of liquidated damages that will be charged for each day the job progresses past the required completion date. This report will be your greatest aid in determining the schedule and cost to complete a job.

To access the Estimated Time and Costs Report for a job, click on the "More Info" link on the "Choose Job for Bid" screen. To view the Estimated Time and Cost report for a job you are currently working on, click the "More Info" link on the Progress Report.

TIP: The Estimated Time and Cost report is updated each period for jobs you are currently working on, so be sure to check it each period. It can help you determine if your job will go over budget or miss its required completion date.
