4.4 Market Analysis

This report will simply tell you the number of new jobs that will be available for bid in the next period. This information may be helpful if you cannot decide whether or not to bid on a job during the current period. If you buy this report and find there will be many jobs available in the next period, you may decide not to bid in the current period with the hope that there will be a more attractive job available next period. If you buy the report and find there will be only a few jobs next period, you may choose to bid on the job now because it is less likely a better job will be available next period.

To view a market analysis report, select "Market Analysis of Future Construction Demand" from the Main Menu. If you do not want to view the report you may select the back button. You must agree to pay the price by selecting 'Yes' before you may view the report.
