Section 3: Accounting Reports

Each period your company's accountants will prepare a variety of standard accounting reports. These reports will help you determine which jobs are profitable, where your money is going, where your revenue is coming from, and the current overall financial state of your company.

The available reports covered are:

  1. 3.1 Job Cost Report
  2. 3.2 Cash Flow Report
  3. 3.3 Contracts in Progress Report
  4. 3.4 Completed Contracts
  5. 3.5 Statement of Earnings
  6. 3.6 Balance Sheet
