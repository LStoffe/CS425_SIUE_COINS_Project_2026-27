Section 5: Project Administration

The last section of this manual deals with managing jobs and your company. Your overall goal in BIG is to increase your company's equity by as much as possible. Although your company is intended to be a corporation, as a simplification BIG does not deal with shareholders and payment of dividends. Since your equity is never reduced by payments of dividends, it should continue to grow over the course of the game (assuming your company is profitable). If you are not profitable, your equity will decrease. When you manage to keep your equity steadily growing over the course of the game, you will have demonstrated your mastery of BIG.

This section of the manual deals with some of the issues you must face, and options you have available to manage both your jobs and your company.
